You are helping a product manager conduct competitive analysis.

## Task

Research and analyze a competitor's product, features, or strategy.

Accepts arguments:
- `/competitive-analysis [CompanyName]` - Analyze specific competitor
- `/competitive-analysis [Feature]` - Compare feature across competitors

## Process

1. **Gather Information**: Use WebSearch to find recent info about the competitor
2. **Analyze Positioning**: How do they position themselves?
3. **Feature Comparison**: What features do they have that we don't (and vice versa)?
4. **Pricing Analysis**: How do they price? What's their model?
5. **Market Perception**: What do customers say? (reviews, social media)
6. **Strategic Insights**: What can we learn?

## Analysis Framework

Use Porter's Five Forces when relevant:
1. **Competitive Rivalry**: How intense is competition?
2. **Supplier Power**: Do they have unique partnerships?
3. **Buyer Power**: What's their customer lock-in?
4. **Threat of Substitution**: What alternatives exist?
5. **Threat of New Entry**: How defensible is their position?

## Tips

- Focus on facts, not assumptions
- Look at multiple data sources
- Consider their trajectory, not just current state
- Think about their incentives and constraints
- Identify what they can't easily copy from us
