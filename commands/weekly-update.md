You are helping a product manager create their weekly update for leadership and stakeholders.

## Task

Create a comprehensive yet concise weekly update that communicates progress, plans, and blockers.

## Process

1. Review the team's Jira board (use Atlassian MCP if configured)
2. Review any major issues in your Amplitude dashboard (use Amplitude MCP if configured)
3. Check for completed user stories or features
4. Look for project documentation or status files
5. Identify any metrics or KPIs if available
6. Compile into executive-friendly format

## Output Format

```markdown
## Weekly Update - Week of [Date]

### Highlights
- [Major achievement or milestone]
- [Key decision or launch]
- [Important metric movement]

### Progress This Week

#### Completed
- [Feature/Story] - [Impact/Value]
- [Feature/Story] - [Impact/Value]

#### In Progress
- [Feature/Story] - [% complete or status]
- [Feature/Story] - [% complete or status]

### Metrics
| Metric | This Week | Last Week | Change |
|--------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| [Metric 1] | [Value] | [Value] | [+/- %] |
| [Metric 2] | [Value] | [Value] | [+/- %] |

### Next Week's Priorities
1. [Priority 1]
2. [Priority 2]
3. [Priority 3]

### Blockers & Risks
- **[Blocker]**: [Description] - *Needs*: [What's needed to unblock]
- **[Risk]**: [Description] - *Mitigation*: [Plan]

### Asks / Support Needed
- [Request 1]
- [Request 2]
```

## Style Guidelines

- Lead with wins and positive momentum
- Be specific with metrics and outcomes
- Highlight customer/business impact
- Keep blockers factual, not emotional
- Make asks clear and actionable
- Use bullet points for scannability
- Maximum 1 page

## Tone

- Confident but transparent
- Data-driven where possible
- Solution-oriented on problems
- Forward-looking
