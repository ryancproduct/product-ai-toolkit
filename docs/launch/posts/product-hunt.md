# Product Hunt Listing

**Tagline (60 chars):**
AI operating system for Product Management

**Description:**
Open-source AI toolkit built on Claude Code with 15 commands, 14+ skills, 9 specialist agents, and deep integrations with PM tools (Amplitude, Jira, Figma, Slack, Confluence). Encodes real PM frameworks into AI workflows with parallel agent dispatch and MCP-grounded analysis.

**Topics:** Productivity, Artificial Intelligence, Open Source, Product Management

**Maker Comment:**

Hey Product Hunt — I'm Ryan, a Principal PM at SafetyCulture.

I built AI PM Kit because most AI tools for PMs are either too generic or too narrow. Nothing that mirrors how we actually work.

**What makes this different:**

- Real PM frameworks (Jobs-to-be-Done, Teresa Torres, INVEST) encoded into workflows
- Grounded in your actual data — pulls from Amplitude, Jira, Figma, Slack, Confluence via MCP
- Parallel specialist agents that work like cross-functional stakeholders
- 15 practical commands for discovery, strategy, experiments, and analysis

Example: `/debate "Add a bulk actions feature"` spawns Champion and Sceptic agents that argue using your real analytics data, then synthesise into a risk register and go/no-go recommendation.

It's open source because I think this pattern (AI + frameworks + real data) is more valuable as a community resource than a SaaS product.

Built on Claude Code. Works locally. No data leaves your machine.

Would love your feedback — which PM workflows would be most valuable to add next?
