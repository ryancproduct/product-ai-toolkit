# Conference CFP: Talk Submission

## Talk Title

**Building an AI Operating System for Product Management**

## Abstract

Product managers spend hours each week on high-value but repetitive work: synthesising user feedback, writing PRDs, running competitive analysis, designing experiments, updating stakeholders. These tasks require judgement but follow known patterns. They're perfect candidates for AI augmentation — yet most AI tools for PMs are either too generic or too narrow.

This talk presents a different approach: encoding PM frameworks and workflows directly into an AI agent system. I'll share how I built AI PM Kit, an open-source "AI operating system" for product management, using Claude Code's agent architecture.

**What you'll see:**

- How specialist agents (strategist, researcher, analyst, designer) can be orchestrated in parallel to handle complex PM workflows
- How grounding agents in live product data (via MCP integrations to Amplitude, Jira, Confluence) produces better outputs than prompt engineering alone
- How domain frameworks like Jobs-to-be-Done and Opportunity Solution Trees can be encoded as agent behaviours, not just reference material
- Real examples: stress-testing a feature idea with parallel agents and getting a risk register in minutes, or designing a full A/B test with validated analytics events

This isn't about replacing PMs. It's about giving them leverage. The work still requires judgement, but the tedious parts — synthesis, formatting, context-gathering — are automated.

You'll walk away with a mental model for building AI tools that respect domain expertise, plus a working open-source system you can use and adapt.

## Speaker Bio

Ryan Clement is a Principal Product Manager at SafetyCulture, where he builds tools used by millions of frontline workers across safety, quality, and operations. He's spent years shipping products in enterprise SaaS and has become focused on how AI can amplify (not replace) expert judgement. AI PM Kit is his open-source attempt to give other PMs the same leverage he wished he'd had years ago. He writes and prototypes in public, optimising for learning speed over polish.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Practical AI architecture patterns** — How to structure specialist agents, orchestrate parallel workflows, and ground agents in live data sources
2. **Framework encoding as a design approach** — Moving beyond prompts to encode domain expertise directly into agent behaviours
3. **A working open-source system** — Attendees leave with access to AI PM Kit, a production-ready toolkit they can use and customise immediately
4. **Leverage vs replacement** — A clear mental model for where AI adds value in knowledge work, and where human judgement remains essential
