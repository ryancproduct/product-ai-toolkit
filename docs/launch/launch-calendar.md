## Notes on Strategy

- **Authenticity over scale** — Share genuine learnings and workflows, not marketing copy
- **Community-first** — This is an open-source contribution, not a product launch. Lead with value
- **Respond generously** — Every comment is an opportunity to refine the kit and build relationships
- **Document learnings** — Each interaction surfaces ways to improve the templates and documentation
- **Be patient** — Open-source adoption is gradual. Focus on finding the right users, not the most users
